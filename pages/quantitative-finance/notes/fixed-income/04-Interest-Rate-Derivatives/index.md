# 04 — Interest Rate Derivatives

Value forward-rate agreements, fixed-for-floating swaps, caps, floors and swaptions using bond replication, forward measures and annuity numeraires.

The one-curve par swap rate is

$$
S(t;T_0,T_n)=\frac{P(t,T_0)-P(t,T_n)}{\sum_{k=1}^{n}\delta_kP(t,T_k)}.
$$
