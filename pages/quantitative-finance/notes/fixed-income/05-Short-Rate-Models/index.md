# 05 — Short-Rate Models

Study the risk-neutral short-rate pricing PDE and affine models, including Vasicek, CIR and Hull–White extensions and initial term-structure calibration.

A one-factor risk-neutral short-rate bond value solves

$$
F_t+\mu^{\mathbb Q}F_x+\tfrac12\sigma^2F_{xx}-xF=0,
\qquad F(T,x)=1.
$$
