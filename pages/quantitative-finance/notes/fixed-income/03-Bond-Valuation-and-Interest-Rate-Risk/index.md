# 03 — Bond Valuation and Interest Rate Risk

Price coupon bonds, define their continuously compounded yield, derive duration and convexity, and extend the same sensitivities to portfolio-level risk.

For the bond price as a function of continuously compounded yield,

$$
D(y)=-\frac{B'(y)}{B(y)},
\qquad C(y)=\frac{B''(y)}{B(y)}.
$$
