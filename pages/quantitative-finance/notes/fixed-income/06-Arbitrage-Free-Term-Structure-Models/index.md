# 06 — Arbitrage-Free Term-Structure Models

Derive change-of-numéraire valuation, the HJM no-arbitrage drift restriction, and the construction of a consistent forward-LIBOR market model.

HJM imposes the risk-neutral forward-rate drift

$$
\alpha^{\mathbb Q}(t,T)
=\boldsymbol\sigma(t,T)\cdot\int_t^T\boldsymbol\sigma(t,u)\,\mathrm{d}u.
$$
