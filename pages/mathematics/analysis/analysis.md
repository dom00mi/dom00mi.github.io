## 1. Introduction

A sequence is a function from the natural numbers to a set,
typically the real numbers.

## 2. Convergence

A sequence $(a_n)$ converges to $L \in \mathbb{R}$ if

$$
\forall \varepsilon > 0,\ \exists N \in \mathbb{N}
\text{ such that }
n \geq N \implies |a_n-L| < \varepsilon.
$$
