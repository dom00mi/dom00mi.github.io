## Core textbooks

1. **Abbott, S.** (2015). *Understanding Analysis* (2nd ed.). Springer.  
   A clear introduction to the real numbers, completeness, and rigorous proof techniques.

2. **Rudin, W.** (1976). *Principles of Mathematical Analysis* (3rd ed.). McGraw-Hill.  
   A concise, rigorous reference for ordered fields, the real number system, and foundational analysis.

3. **Bartle, R. G., & Sherbert, D. R.** (2011). *Introduction to Real Analysis* (4th ed.). Wiley.  
   Detailed treatment of real-number properties, bounds, and elementary real analysis.

## Further reading

4. **Tao, T.** (2016). *Analysis I* (3rd ed.). Hindustan Book Agency / Springer.  
   Develops number systems and real analysis carefully from foundational principles.

5. **Apostol, T. M.** (1974). *Mathematical Analysis* (2nd ed.). Addison-Wesley.  
   A broader treatment of mathematical analysis and its underlying structures.

## Suggested reading path

For a first pass, begin with **Abbott** or **Bartle and Sherbert**. Use **Rudin** for a more compact, theorem-driven treatment. Consult **Tao** if you want to understand the construction of the real numbers in greater foundational detail.
