## Core textbooks

1. **Howie, J. M.** (2001). *Real Analysis*. Springer Undergraduate Mathematics Series. Springer.<br>
   A concise introduction to sequences, limits, continuity, and the basic structure of real analysis.

2. **Protter, M. H., &amp; Morrey, C. B.** (1991). *A First Course in Real Analysis*. Springer.<br>
   A systematic treatment of convergence and the foundational ideas of one-variable analysis.

3. **Ross, K. A.** (1980). *Elementary Analysis: The Theory of Calculus*. Springer.<br>
   A clear development of sequences, limits, series, and the theory underlying differential and integral calculus.

## Suggested reading path

Begin with **Howie** for a concise introduction to the central definitions and examples. Use **Protter and Morrey** for a more systematic treatment, and consult **Ross** for additional perspectives connecting sequences and limits with the broader theory of calculus.
