## Core textbooks

1. Jim Hefferon, *Linear Algebra*, 4th ed. (2020). Free open textbook and source materials: [hefferon.net/linearalgebra](https://hefferon.net/linearalgebra/).

2. David Cherney, Tom Denton, Rohit Thomas, and Andrew Waldron, *Linear Algebra*, 1st ed. (University of California, Davis, 2013; online text updated subsequently). [Official UC Davis text](https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~linear/).

3. Howard Anton, Chris Rorres, and Anton Kaul, *Elementary Linear Algebra: Applications Version*, 12th ed. (Wiley, 2019). [Publisher information](https://www.wiley.com/en-us/Elementary+Linear+Algebra%2C+Applications+Version%2C+12th+Edition-p-9781119282365).

4. Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe, *Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra: Vectors, Matrices, and Least Squares* (Cambridge University Press, 2018). [Author-maintained book site](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/vmls/) and [Cambridge University Press](https://doi.org/10.1017/9781108583664).

## How these references support the chapter

- Hefferon provides a rigorous undergraduate treatment of systems, vector spaces, linear maps, determinants, and eigenvalues.
- Cherney, Denton, Thomas, and Waldron provide a complementary, application-oriented first course with an emphasis on conceptual connections.
- Anton, Rorres, and Kaul provide a conventional elementary treatment with extensive computational examples and applications.
- Boyd and Vandenberghe provide the applied and data-science perspective, especially for vectors, matrices, least squares, and numerical modeling.
