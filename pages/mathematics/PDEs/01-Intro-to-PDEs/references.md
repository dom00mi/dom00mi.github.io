# References — Partial Differential Equations

The following references provide historical context, theoretical foundations, and further reading for the study of partial differential equations.

## 1. Historical Perspectives and Quotations

### Felix Klein

Klein emphasized the central role of differential equations in the development of modern mathematics.

- Klein, F. (1893). *The Evanston Colloquium: Lectures on Mathematics*. Macmillan, 1894.

  [Historical discussion](https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2010/11/11/the-grand-unified-theory-of-19th-century-math/)

### Alan Turing

The remark comparing science to a differential equation and religion to a boundary condition appeared in a postcard sent by Turing to Robin Gandy in March 1954.

- Hodges, A. (1983). *Alan Turing: The Enigma*. Simon & Schuster. See the discussion of Turing's correspondence with Robin Gandy, pp. 512–514 in the cited edition.

  [Quotation and historical verification — Quote Investigator](https://quoteinvestigator.com/2023/01/05/boundary/)

### George Pólya

Pólya's reflections on mathematical problem-solving highlight the importance of intuition, understanding, and the development of suitable methods.

- Pólya, G. (1945). *How to Solve It: A New Aspect of Mathematical Method*. Princeton University Press.

  [Selected quotations — MacTutor, University of St Andrews](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Polya/quotations/)

### Peter D. Lax and Louis Nirenberg

Their contributions helped shape the modern theory of partial differential equations.

- The Abel Prize (2005). *Peter D. Lax — Laureate Citation*.

  [Peter D. Lax — Abel Prize](https://abelprize.no/abel-prize-laureates/2005)

- The Abel Prize (2015). *John F. Nash Jr. and Louis Nirenberg — Laureate Information*.

  [The Abel Prize](https://abelprize.no/)

## 2. Foundational Textbooks

### Lawrence C. Evans

Evans, L. C. (2010). *Partial Differential Equations*. 2nd ed., Graduate Studies in Mathematics, Vol. 19. American Mathematical Society.

A comprehensive reference for classical and modern PDE theory, including weak solutions, Sobolev spaces, elliptic equations, and evolution equations.

### Fritz John

John, F. (1982). *Partial Differential Equations*. 4th ed., Applied Mathematical Sciences, Vol. 1. Springer.

A classical introduction to PDEs, with particular attention to fundamental solution techniques and mathematical physics.

### Walter A. Strauss

Strauss, W. A. (2008). *Partial Differential Equations: An Introduction*. 2nd ed., Wiley.

An accessible introduction to the heat, wave, and Laplace equations, including separation of variables and Fourier methods.

### Michael E. Taylor

Taylor, M. E. (2011). *Partial Differential Equations I: Basic Theory*. 2nd ed., Applied Mathematical Sciences, Vol. 115. Springer.

A more advanced treatment of the analytical foundations of PDE theory.
