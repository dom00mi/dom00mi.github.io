## From Basic Probability to Advanced Stochastic Analysis

The development of modern probability theory owes much to **Andrey Nikolaevich Kolmogorov**, whose axiomatic formulation in 1933 provided the rigorous mathematical foundations upon which much of the subject is built today. By establishing a precise framework for studying randomness, Kolmogorov made it possible to move beyond simple probability experiments towards the analysis of increasingly complex stochastic phenomena.

These notes are intended **purely for educational purposes**, following a journey from the basic principles of probability to more advanced topics in stochastic analysis and the mathematical modelling of random processes.

The idea behind this collection is simple: to revisit the foundations, build a deeper understanding along the way, and gradually connect the theory to more sophisticated mathematical concepts. I believe that even the most advanced ideas become more approachable when we take the time to understand where they come from and why they matter.

Beyond serving as a personal reference, I hope these notes might also encourage others to explore the subject, ask questions, and develop their own curiosity about the mathematics of randomness. They are not meant to replace textbooks or offer an exhaustive treatment, but rather to provide another perspective on a subject that I find both challenging and fascinating.
