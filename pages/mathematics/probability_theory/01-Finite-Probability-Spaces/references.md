# References — Finite Probability Spaces

## Overview

Here, we collect textbooks and academic references covering the foundations of probability theory, including finite probability spaces, probability measures, $\sigma$-algebras, filtrations, random variables, and probability distributions.

The references range from introductory treatments of elementary probability to more rigorous approaches based on measure theory and stochastic processes.

This is particularly good for anyone that is starting to dive into those topics, or get a broader understanding! Here we go!

---

## 1. Introductory Probability

The following textbooks provide an accessible introduction to probability theory, with particular emphasis on finite sample spaces, combinatorial methods, random variables, and elementary probability distributions.

**[1] Stirzaker, D. (2003).**  
*Elementary Probability.*  
Cambridge University Press.

**[2] Ross, S. M. (1998).**  
*A First Course in Probability.*  
Prentice Hall.

**[3] Haigh, J. (2013).**  
*Probability Models.*  
Springer.

These books are useful for developing intuition about random experiments and probability models before moving to the more abstract measure-theoretic framework.

---

## 2. Measure-Theoretic Probability

The following references develop probability theory through the mathematical framework of measurable spaces, $\sigma$-algebras, probability measures, and measurable functions.

**[4] Billingsley, P. (2012).**  
*Probability and Measure.*  
Anniversary Edition. Wiley Series in Probability and Statistics.  
John Wiley & Sons.

**[5] Capiński, M., & Kopp, E. (2004).**  
*Measure, Integral and Probability.*  
2nd Edition. Springer Undergraduate Mathematics Series.  
Springer-Verlag, London.

**[6] Çınlar, E. (2011).**  
*Probability and Stochastics.*  
Graduate Texts in Mathematics, Vol. 261.  
Springer, New York.

**[7] Chung, K. L. (2001).**  
*A Course in Probability Theory.*  
3rd Edition. Academic Press.

**[8] Durrett, R. (2019).**  
*Probability: Theory and Examples.*  
5th Edition. Cambridge Series in Statistical and Probabilistic Mathematics, Vol. 49.  
Cambridge University Press.

These texts are particularly relevant for understanding the formal construction of probability spaces and the mathematical foundations required for advanced probability theory.

---

## 3. Probability and Stochastic Processes

The following books extend the foundational concepts of probability to stochastic processes, filtrations, and information evolving over time.

**[9] Grimmett, G. R., & Stirzaker, D. R. (2020).**  
*Probability and Random Processes.*  
4th Edition. Oxford University Press.

**[10] Jacod, J., & Protter, P. (2004).**  
*Probability Essentials.*  
2nd Edition. Universitext. Springer-Verlag, Berlin.

These references are particularly useful when progressing from finite probability spaces to stochastic processes, conditional expectations, martingales, and stochastic calculus.

---

## 4. Suggested Reading Order

For readers approaching probability theory from the beginning, the following progression is recommended:

1. **Elementary Probability:** Start with Stirzaker [1] or Ross [2] to develop familiarity with probability spaces, events, and random variables.

2. **Mathematical Foundations:** Continue with Capiński and Kopp [5] to introduce measure theory and the formal mathematical structure of probability.

3. **Rigorous Probability Theory:** Study Billingsley [4] or Durrett [8] for a deeper treatment of probability measures, convergence, and random variables.

4. **Stochastic Processes:** Progress to Grimmett and Stirzaker [9], Çınlar [6], or Jacod and Protter [10] for more advanced probabilistic concepts.
